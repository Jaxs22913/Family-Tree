Why only these seven are hosted here. Everything on this page is a United States federal record or a Georgia state record — public documents, free to reproduce. They are copied from the National Archives and the Georgia Archives and served from this site, so they open instantly and will still be here if those sites change.

Headstone photographs belong to the people who took them, and the newspaper archive sits behind an access challenge. Those are linked rather than copied, and quoted where the words matter — which for a newspaper is usually the point anyway.
